# Fitzo 1.4.0 release — 12 October 2026

## Included changes

- Short training-first setup: Goal → Routine → Plan. Body measurements and food targets are optional.
- Previewed starter workouts based on equipment, experience and available time. Suggestions never create completed sets or performed workout history.
- Editable training preferences with separate keep/replace choices and retained workout history.
- Faint screen graphics, clearer empty/error states, optional recovery check-ins and detailed health sync status.
- Eight practical meal ideas, including six vegetarian ideas. The saved vegetarian preference is the default; the member can choose all meals explicitly.
- Ready-to-eat portions in grams and household/count measures, half-portion adjustments, removable foods and reversible substitutions. Swaps reset to the new food's base portion and update nutrition before logging.
- Meal category selection, reviewed portions saved in `calorie_logs.serving_size`, and estimates marked in `calorie_logs.is_estimate`.
- Meal draft preservation after failures, immediate double-tap protection and account-bound writes. Browsing ideas creates no food logs.

## Nutrition basis and limits

The meal ideas reference Fitzo's existing Indian food catalogue, using the same nutrition values as search instead of a second set of guessed macros. These are ordinary starting portions, not personalised meal prescriptions or a promise to meet a daily calorie/protein target. Recipes, oil and household cup sizes vary; the UI labels estimates and asks the member to review portions. Plain paneer explicitly excludes added cooking oil. The existing target formula is unchanged.

The [ICMR-NIN dietary guidelines](https://www.nin.res.in/dietaryguidelines/pdfjs/locale/DGI07052024P.pdf) informed the mix of familiar food groups. Their daily raw-food quantities are not used as cooked per-meal portions. Grocery lists and direct coach actions remain future work.
