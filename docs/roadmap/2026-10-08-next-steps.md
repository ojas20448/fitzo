## Starting-template rationale

The [ACSM 2026 update](https://acsm.org/resistance-training-guidelines-update-2026/) supports simple, consistent resistance training and recognises home/bodyweight training. Fitzo's bounded templates are a product starting point, not a validated personalised programme or a guarantee of muscle gain. They use two sets for new/returning users and short sessions; regular adult users can receive three sets for longer sessions. No loads, one-rep maximums, automatic progression or fixed outcomes are invented. Users under 18 keep two sets. No-equipment plans disclose limited pulling options. The time choice limits exercise count and is labelled approximate.

Manager features remain deferred; staging remains out of scope as requested.
