Borrow the clarity.

Repivo shows a focused entry point, stable navigation and controls grouped around the task. Fitzo can apply those principles while retaining food logging, its existing draft recovery and previous-session data.

Measure the outcome.

First compare task success, time to first set and repeat-meal effort. Then measure meaningful D7/D30 returns and weekly logging, alongside failed saves and draft recovery. The recording alone cannot establish retention.
